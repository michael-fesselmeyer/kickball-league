# Dry run, November 2026: races and double actions

A hands-on pass over the app with seeded demo data and stripe-mock, looking
for bugs and race conditions that would hurt players, captains, refs and
admins. Every finding is codified as a test. **Status: in progress** (paused
for machine load; the remaining plan is at the end).

## Findings

| # | Area | What went wrong | Status | Test |
|---|------|-----------------|--------|------|
| 1 | Payments | A second "Pay" (another tab, or the back button after paying) expired the first checkout at Stripe and closed its purchase here even when Stripe refused because that checkout was already paid. The player could pay twice, and league credit held by the first checkout was given back although it was spent. | Fixed | `src/services/stale-checkouts.test.ts` |
| 2 | Event tickets | Returning to a ticket checkout when Stripe could not be reached closed the purchase and freed its spot, although the checkout might still be paid. | Fixed | `src/services/stale-checkouts.test.ts` |
| 3 | Schedule | Two admins publishing different drafts at the same moment: the second hit the one-published index and saw an error page. | Fixed (publishes of a season take turns) | `src/db/queries/schedule-races.db.test.ts` |
| 4 | Schedule | Publishing a draft made before another version went out (for example before a rain-out's makeups were published) quietly undid that version, putting games back into called-off slots. | Fixed (refused as stale; a generated draft follows the published version) | `src/db/queries/schedule-races.db.test.ts` |
| 5 | Playoffs | Two playoff results recorded at once each added their next game to the published schedule from the same version; the second publish dropped the first game's slot. | Fixed (under the season's publish lock) | Covered by the lock in 3; a dedicated two-result test is still to write |

### Behaviour change

A duplicate rain-out draft published after the first is now refused as stale
instead of publishing with no changes (`rainout-notices.db.test.ts`). It still
sends nothing.

## Checked and sound

These were tried on purpose and hold, most with existing race tests:

- Two captains on the last sub spot, and two captains answering one claim (`subs.db.test.ts`).
- Two refs taking one game (the game row is locked in `takeRefereeGame`).
- A score confirm racing a dispute, and two people changing a score at once (`scores.db.test.ts`).
- Offline scores replayed, resent, or arriving after someone else's (`offline-scores.db.test.ts`).
- Double-tapped registration and team names (`registration-races.db.test.ts`).
- Stripe webhooks retried (recorded once by event id) and an `expired` event after `completed` (only open purchases change).
- Two captains saving the kicking order at once: the last save wins whole, never a mix.
- A crawl of every linked page at phone width as player (25 pages), captain (69), ref (61) and guest (100): no errors, broken links, overflow or broken text. Public game pages are fast when fetched directly (under 300 ms in dev).

## Notes for later

- `npm run demo:reset` logs pg's "client.query() when the client is already executing a query" deprecation: some seed step runs queries concurrently on one client, which pg 9 will refuse.
- Dev mode logs "encountered URL data during prerendering" for the public schedule, standings, team, bracket and help pages. Dev-only warnings, listed for the performance owners.
